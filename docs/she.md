# SHE

The Scientific Health Engine builds validated substrates and denominator fields. Population reconstruction supports projected-gradient fixtures and memory-preflighted sparse block-coordinate regional/national execution with closure, hard-anchor, migration, aging, birth, death-prior, and race-composition terms.

ST-DFM runs through the compute backend and emits factors, loadings, reconstruction, uncertainty, certification, objective trace, and solver telemetry. Certification states are `verified`, `uncertified`, `fragile`, `failed_certification`, and `blocked_invalid_support`.
