## 1. Headline

- **24,628 pancreatic-cancer deaths** over 2021–2022 (0.729% of all 3,376,915 deaths).
- **Crude mortality 6.08 / 100 000 / yr.**

## 12. Named limitations

- **No smoking covariate** exists in SIDRA (the dominant modifiable C25 risk factor); tobacco-attributable fraction requires VIGITEL/PNS, outside this pipeline.
- **Two-year window (2021–2022)** — temporal-trend/APC analysis needs the full 1996–2023 SIM fetch (authored as `config/intents/national_sim_fullrange_fetch.json`; the multi-hour fetch must run in a persistent terminal — the agent's background execution could not persist it on Windows).
- **Socio-economic determinants** (income/education/sanitation via the 94-table SIDRA compendium) require the contextual investigate run (`national_pancreatic_c25_determinants` intent); the LDO here reports associations *among mortality causes*, net of confounders.
- Rates are by municipality of **residence**; the very smallest municipalities have unstable rates (reported crude, not standardized, below a count threshold).
